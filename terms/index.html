5. Wikis, trial period and expiry

A new wiki is normally a trial with an end date. Unless the portal shows otherwise when you create it, the term is 14 days. The dashboard shows the time left for each wiki. If the portal says, when you create a wiki, that it has no end date, the wiki does not expire and the end-date rules below do not apply to it.

  • We email the owner 3 days and 1 day before the end date, if the owner’s account has an email address.
  • Where the portal offers renewal, the owner, or a collaborator allowed to start and stop the wiki, can renew it during its last 7 days; the wiki then gets a new full term counted from the renewal. Otherwise you can ask for an extension by writing to contact@bananawiki.com with your username and the wiki’s address. We decide on extensions case by case, and we will tell you why if we refuse.
  • On the end date the wiki is stopped and terminated, and the owner is told by email. Its data is kept for a grace period of 30 days, during which we can restore it on request. After the grace period it is permanently deleted.

Keep each wiki within its storage limit. A wiki that exceeds its limit by more than 10% is suspended automatically; the portal shows the reason, and we email the owner. It reopens automatically as soon as it fits within its limit again, and we email the owner when it does. Write to us if you need room to free space: we may raise its limit for a short time, and the wiki reopens as soon as it fits within the raised limit.

Declare each wiki’s intended use accurately when you create it, and keep that declaration up to date.

If you transfer a wiki to another account, you lose access to it when the new owner accepts, unless they choose to keep you as a viewer.

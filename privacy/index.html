Privacy Policy

BananaWiki Hosting · Version 2026-10-09

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use BananaWiki Hosting (the portal at hosting.bananawiki.com and the wikis it hosts) and this website. It is the information required by Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the GDPR) and by the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003, as amended by Legislative Decree 101/2018). It does not cover copies of BananaWiki that other people install on their own servers.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Luca Zani, who runs the service (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at contact@bananawiki.com.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer: Article 37 GDPR does not require one for this processing, which involves no large-scale monitoring and no special categories of data. Write to the address above about anything in this policy.

2. Our role for each kind of data

We are the controller of the data about hosting accounts and their wikis, the security and moderation records of the service, our correspondence with you, and this website.

We are a processor for what is inside a hosted wiki: its pages, files, comments, messages and boards, and the accounts and activity records of the people who use it. The owner of the wiki (usually the school, team or organisation that created it) is the controller of that data and decides who may use the wiki and why. We process it only on the owner’s behalf, under the data processing terms in section 16 of the Terms of Service. If you are a member of a wiki hosted here, contact that wiki’s administrators first; requests we receive about a wiki’s content are passed on to its owner.

3. What data we process

  • Account data: username; email address, when it was set, whether it is verified and whether it was reported as not working; password, stored only as a salted hash; creation date; account status (approval, suspension, scheduled deletion and the reasons given); the date and version of the terms you accepted; the intended use you describe when signing up; language and theme preferences.
  • Data about your wikis: address and any custom domain; status, creation and expiry dates; storage used; the declared purpose; requests you make (for example public reading, the page builder, an extension or an ownership transfer); and the people you invite to help manage them.
  • Security data: for each sign-in session, the IP address at sign-in and the most recent one, the browser’s user-agent string, times and the sign-in method; failed sign-in attempts and rate-limit counters, keyed to an IP address, an account or a browser; two-step verification data (the authenticator secret is stored encrypted, recovery codes only as hashes); personal API tokens, stored only as hashes.
  • Moderation and audit records: actions on accounts and wikis (approval, suspension, termination, deletion, ownership transfers, and an administrator signing in as you to give support, and your acceptance of new terms), who took them, when, and the reason recorded.
  • Email records: for each email the service sends, the recipient, the type of message, the time and the account that caused it, used to enforce sending limits.
  • Sign-in to hosted wikis: when you sign in to a hosted wiki with your hosting account, the wiki receives your account identifier, username and account creation date, and we record which wiki username your hosting account is linked to.
  • Correspondence and notices: what you write to us, and the reports of illegal content and complaints we receive, including the reporter’s name and email address when given.
  • Server logs: the web and application servers record the IP address, time, requested address, response status and user-agent of requests, and errors.
  • Wiki content: whatever the owner and members of a hosted wiki store in it, processed as described in section 2.

Most of this data comes from you. Membership and content of a hosted wiki come from its owner and members; reports of illegal content come from the people who send them. Do not include special categories of data (such as health, religion or political opinions) in your sign-up description or in requests to us.

A username, a password and, where the portal asks for it, a working email address are needed to create an account. Without them we cannot provide the service.

This website (bananawiki.com) is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Its host records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs.

5. Who receives the data

Personal data is handled by the people who run the service for us, who are bound to confidentiality and act on our instructions (Art. 29 GDPR and Art. 2-quaterdecies of the Privacy Code). We use these processors, bound by data processing agreements under Art. 28 GDPR:

  • Hosting: the server provider, which provides the servers and storage, in a data centre in the European Union.
  • Email delivery: the email service in use, which delivers the service’s emails and receives the recipient address and the message.
  • Off-site backups: the storage services that hold our backups, which store encrypted backup files only. The files are encrypted on our servers before upload, and the storage provider does not have the key.
  • This website: the provider that serves bananawiki.com.

Other recipients, each acting as an independent controller:

  • the owner and administrators of a hosted wiki, for the accounts and content of that wiki; the people you invite to manage one of your wikis see your username;
  • courts, police and other public authorities, when the law requires it, including orders under Articles 9 and 10 of the Digital Services Act and notifications of suspected crimes threatening life or safety under Article 18;
  • professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, bound to confidentiality, where needed;
  • a successor that takes over the service, after notice to you as described in the Terms.

Pages in a hosted wiki can embed videos from YouTube (through youtube-nocookie.com) or Vimeo when the wiki’s authors add them. When you open such a page, your browser connects to that service, which receives your IP address and applies its own privacy policy. The wiki’s owner decides whether to embed videos.

6. Transfers outside the EU

The service’s servers and storage are in the European Union. Data leaves the European Economic Area only where a processor listed in section 5 is established outside it or uses its own sub-processors there. This can apply to the email service, the off-site backup storage and the website’s host. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795) for certified recipients, or on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914. Backups are encrypted before they leave our servers. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards used.

8. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, its correction, its erasure, the restriction of its processing and a copy in a portable format, and you can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your situation.

Much of this you can do yourself in the portal: change your email address and password and download your account data (Download my data) on the Account page; see and end your sign-in sessions, and clear the history of ended ones; as the owner of a wiki, export the whole wiki from its Administration, Site migration page; and delete your account.

For anything else, write to contact@bananawiki.com. We reply free of charge within one month, which can be extended by two further months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the email address on your account. The rights of a person who has died can be exercised by someone with an interest of their own, or acting to protect that person or for family reasons (Art. 2-terdecies of the Privacy Code).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Roma, www.garanteprivacy.it, email protocollo@gpdp.it, or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR). You can also go to court (Art. 79 GDPR).

9. Cookies and browser storage

The service uses only technical cookies, which are strictly necessary for signing in, for security or to remember a choice you made. There are no analytics, advertising or profiling cookies, and no third-party cookies set by us. Under Art. 122 of the Privacy Code and the Garante’s Guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021, technical cookies do not require consent, so there is no cookie banner. This website sets no cookies at all.

10. Children

In Italy, the minimum age to consent on one’s own to the processing of personal data for online services is 14 (Art. 2-quinquies of the Privacy Code). You must be at least 14 to open a hosting account, as the Terms explain. Schools and other organisations may let younger pupils use a wiki they host here; they are the controller for those pupils’ data and are responsible for the legal basis, including the consent of parents or guardians where needed. If we learn that a hosting account belongs to someone under 14, we will delete it.

11. Security

We protect personal data with measures suited to the risk (Art. 32 GDPR): encrypted connections (HTTPS); passwords, recovery codes and tokens stored only as hashes and authenticator secrets stored encrypted; two-step verification, available to every account and required for our administrators; a separate, resource-limited container for each wiki; rate limits and sign-in throttling; access to production systems limited to the people who run the service; a record of administrative actions, including support sign-ins as another account; encrypted backups; and prompt software updates.

If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the Garante within 72 hours where required (Art. 33) and tell the people affected without undue delay when it is likely to result in a high risk to them (Art. 34). Please report security problems privately to contact@bananawiki.com or as the project’s security policy explains.

12. Changes to this policy

We publish every new version of this policy on this page; the Version line at the top gives its date. Before a material change takes effect, we tell account holders by email or in the portal. Earlier versions are available on request.

This policy is published in English, Italian and German. All versions have the same value; if they differ, the one most favourable to you applies.
